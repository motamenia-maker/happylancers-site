FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free for lancers?

Yes. You keep 100% of what you earn. Pro ($28 a month) adds unlimited AI income plans, auto-apply and earnings reports.

Where is Happy Lancers available?

We are starting with a beta in Miami. Remote gigs work from anywhere in the U.S.; Los Angeles and Las Vegas are next.

What kinds of work can I find?

Local gigs like dog walking, cleaning and tutoring; remote projects like writing, design and voice-over; and short contracts like data or business analysis.

How do clients pay?

Clients pay through the app before the work starts. The money is held and released to the lancer when the job is done.

Is my data safe?

We only use your data to match you with work, with your consent. We never sell it.
